Netflix has unveiled the trailer for Tyler Perry's 'The Gospel of Christmas,' a faith-centered holiday drama arriving November 11, 2026. Written and directed by Perry, the film follows Isaac, a struggling pastor played by Tank, who inherits his family's church in a troubled New York City neighborhood — and must decide what faithfulness looks like when the bills are due and the pews are thin.

Fantasia Barrino plays Isaac's wife, Rebecca, at the center of a family story about holding together when everything feels fragile. Around them gathers an extraordinary cast of gospel music legends: CeCe Winans, Yolanda Adams, Shirley Caesar, Fred Hammond, the Clark Sisters, Hezekiah Walker, Smokie Norful, Jekalyn Carr and Vanessa Bell Armstrong.

The film's music is produced by Kirk Franklin, with a soundtrack blending traditional and contemporary gospel, rap and holiday music. For Perry, the songs are not decoration: he has described songs that helped him through hard times as the starting point of the project, and the film treats gospel music as the language a struggling church speaks when words run out.

'The Gospel of Christmas' adds to Perry's growing slate of faith-centered projects, following his studio's multiyear partnership with Netflix to produce faith-based films. It arrives as part of the streamer's 2026 Christmas lineup, ahead of Thanksgiving.

For families looking for a Christmas film that takes faith seriously — not as background, but as the story itself — November 11 offers a night at home with a pastor, a church and a choir reminding viewers why the church sings, even in a hard season.

A su alrededor se reúne un elenco extraordinario de leyendas de la música gospel: CeCe Winans, Yolanda Adams, Shirley Caesar, Fred Hammond, las Clark Sisters, Hezekiah Walker, Smokie Norful, Jekalyn Carr y Vanessa Bell Armstrong.

'The Gospel of Christmas' se suma a la creciente lista de proyectos centrados en la fe de Perry, tras la alianza multianual de su estudio con Netflix para producir películas basadas en la fe. Llega como parte de la programación navideña 2026 de la plataforma, antes del Día de Acción de Gracias.

Para las familias que buscan una película navideña que tome la fe en serio —no como fondo, sino como la historia misma—, el 11 de noviembre ofrece una noche en casa con un pastor, una iglesia y un coro que recuerda a los espectadores por qué la iglesia canta, incluso en una temporada difícil.
