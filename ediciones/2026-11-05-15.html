"Forgiveness Is Revolutionary": The Smallbone Brothers Reconciled While Making a Film About Reconciliation«El perdón es revolucionario»: los hermanos Smallbone se reconciliaron mientras hacían una película sobre la reconciliación

Ben and Joel Smallbone had a falling-out so bitter they swore never to work together again — then a coffee-shop meeting in Utah healed the rift, and their Revolutionary War Christmas musical Drummer Boy opens November 6.Ben y Joel Smallbone tuvieron un distanciamiento tan amargo que juraron no volver a trabajar juntos, hasta que un encuentro en una cafetería de Utah sanó la ruptura, y su musical navideño de la Guerra de Independencia, Drummer Boy, se estrena el 6 de noviembre.

The film is about brothers reconciling. The irony is that the brothers making it had to live the story first. Filmmaker Ben Smallbone revealed in an interview on November 3, 2026, that he and his brother Joel Smallbone, of the duo for KING + COUNTRY, suffered a serious personal and professional falling-out about three years ago while developing Drummer Boy — a rift so deep that both men said they would never work together again.

The break came during the long development of a project both brothers cared about deeply. Drummer Boy, a Revolutionary War Christmas musical, took eight years to bring to the screen, and somewhere along that road the working relationship between the two brothers fractured. For a time, it seemed the film about reconciliation might never be finished by the very family whose story it mirrored.

The turning point came far from any set. Joel flew to Utah, where Ben was filming the television series Homestead, and the brothers met in a coffee shop. There, away from producers, schedules, and the pressure of the project, they hugged, they cried, they apologized — and they decided to forgive, to reconcile, and to make the film together after all.

That decision is now reaching theaters. Drummer Boy — co-directed by Ben and Joel, starring Joel as Jeremiah Clem and Lucas Leach as Benjamin Clem, with music from for KING + COUNTRY — opens in theaters on November 6, 2026, via Angel Studios. Their brother Luke Smallbone serves as a producer on the film, making the project a true family endeavor in every sense.

Joel's summary of the film could just as easily describe what happened in that Utah coffee shop: "Forgiveness is revolutionary." It is a fitting line for a Christmas musical set amid a war for independence — and for two brothers who discovered that the most revolutionary act of all was choosing each other over being right.
