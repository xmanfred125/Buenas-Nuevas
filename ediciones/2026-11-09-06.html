Argentina's President Milei inaugurates a 4,000-seat evangelical temple in PatagoniaEl presidente Milei inaugura un templo evangélico de 4.000 asientos en la Patagonia

President Javier Milei attended the opening of the new auditorium of Iglesia Bautista Jesús es Rey in Neuquén — the largest temple in Patagonia — on Argentina's National Day of Evangelical and Protestant Churches.El presidente Javier Milei asistió a la inauguración del nuevo auditorio de la Iglesia Bautista Jesús es Rey en Neuquén, el templo más grande de la Patagonia, en el Día Nacional de las Iglesias Evangélicas y Protestantes de Argentina.

President Javier Milei attended the inauguration of a new evangelical temple in Patagonia on Saturday, November 7. The new auditorium of Iglesia Bautista Jesús es Rey in Neuquén seats 4,000 people, making it the largest temple in Patagonia, and it was paid for by the families of the congregation.

The date was no coincidence: the inauguration fell on Argentina's National Day of Evangelical and Protestant Churches, a day that honours the country's Protestant heritage. The church, pastored by Hugo Márquez, is nearly 90 years old, has planted more than 10 churches, runs a school with 1,700 pupils and a clinic with 30 medical specialties.

Milei maintains a close relationship with ACIERA, the Christian Alliance of Evangelical Churches of the Argentine Republic, and his presence in Neuquén continues a pattern. In July 2025, he inaugurated another evangelical temple in Chaco. For a president who speaks openly about his faith, appearing at the opening of a church built by its own congregation is both a personal gesture and a political signal.

The growth on display in Neuquén reflects a wider shift in Argentina. According to a barometer from the University of Buenos Aires, 17.4 percent of Argentines now identify as evangelical — a community that has become impossible for any president to ignore.
