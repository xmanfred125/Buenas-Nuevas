'Beyond Belief' Streams on Angel, with Music from Petra«Beyond Belief» llega en streaming a Angel, con música de Petra

The Christian music drama "Beyond Belief" is now streaming exclusively on Angel, bringing its 1980s story of a teenage rocker, a battle of the bands and the music of Petra to a new audience.El drama musical cristiano «Beyond Belief» ya se transmite en exclusiva en Angel, llevando su historia de los años 80 sobre un rockero adolescente, una batalla de bandas y la música de Petra a un público nuevo.

Abba Post · October 26, 2026Abba Post · 26 de octubre de 2026

"Beyond Belief" is now streaming exclusively on Angel, giving the Christian music-focused film a new home after its theatrical release earlier this year. The 2026 drama is set in the 1980s and follows Andy, a teenage rocker trying to make it in music while dealing with the loss of his father, until a mysterious voice telling him to "run" turns his plans upside down.

The film is set against the world of 1980s rock music, with Andy and his band, Jericho, competing in a battle of the bands. The prize is a chance to open for Petra, bringing the legendary Christian rock band's music directly into the story rather than simply using it as background music.

Carson Lueders stars as Andy, with James C. Burns, Debby Gerber, Ryan Barnes and Libby Smallbone also among the main cast. Johnny Meier directed the film and co-wrote it with Darren Scott Jacobs and Joe Sanders. Petra founder Bob Hartman and lead singer John Schlitt also make cameo appearances, a nod to fans who grew up on the band's anthems.

The film's soundtrack goes beyond the older Petra material too. The official soundtrack contains 12 tracks, including seven original songs created for the movie, three Petra songs, and the opening and closing scores. Lueders performs several of the original songs as part of the fictional band Jericho, and the soundtrack is also available separately, including on CD and vinyl.

For Christian audiences, "Beyond Belief" is both a nostalgia trip and a reminder: the music that shaped a generation's faith still has a story to tell, and a new generation is now hearing it for the first time, not in a record store, but on a streaming screen.
