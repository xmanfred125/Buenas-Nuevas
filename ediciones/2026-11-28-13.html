Beyond Theology: Olivet University Wins Approval for 20 New ProgramsMás allá de la teología: la Universidad Olivet obtiene aprobación para 20 nuevos programas

Olivet University won accreditation approval for 20 new programs, from business and journalism to a doctorate in IT, after moving its main campus to Missouri.La Universidad Olivet obtuvo la aprobación de acreditación para 20 nuevos programas, desde negocios y periodismo hasta un doctorado en TI, tras trasladar su campus principal a Misuri.

November 26, 202626 de noviembre de 2026

Olivet University is growing — and not just in theology. The Association for Biblical Higher Education's Commission on Accreditation has approved 20 new programs for the university, from an English-language certificate to a doctorate in information technology, in a decision recorded on November 10 and reported on November 26.

The new offerings reach well beyond the school's traditional theological focus: bachelor's degrees in business, children's development studies, graphic design, information technology, journalism and music; master's degrees in graphic arts, IT, journalism, music, teaching, and translation and interpretation; plus an MBA, two Master of Divinity tracks — Christian media, and business as mission — and programs in ministry, agriculture, architecture and civil engineering.

The expansion follows the university's move of its main campus from Anza, California, after 22 years, to St. Ann, Missouri, which took effect on June 16, 2026. The 1,000-acre Anza campus remains the flagship, focused on theology, while the move centralises administration for the university's nine U.S. campuses and places it near its denominational affiliate, Olivet Assembly USA. University president Jonathan Park announced the new programs on November 19.

For a school founded to train ministers, the message is clear: the mission has not changed, but the fields where its graduates will serve have grown. From pulpits to newsrooms, from classrooms to construction sites, Olivet's students are being prepared to carry their faith into work the world actually needs.
