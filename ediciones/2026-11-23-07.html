November 22, 202622 de noviembre de 2026

The San Francisco 49ers routed the Minnesota Vikings 35-10 at Estadio Banorte (Estadio Azteca) in Mexico City on Sunday, November 22, 2026, in the NFL's first international Sunday Night Football game and its return to Mexico after a four-year absence.

Brock Purdy completed 21 of 26 passes for 255 yards, three touchdowns and no interceptions, a 146.0 passer rating, with all three touchdown passes coming in the first half. Kirk Cousins, in his second start since being traded back to Minnesota at the deadline, went 26 of 38 for 235 yards, no touchdowns and one interception — an 18-yard pick-six by safety Ji'Ayir Brown just before halftime that made it 28-10.

Christian McCaffrey capped the scoring with a six-yard touchdown run early in the fourth quarter on a 98-yard drive. The 49ers improved to 9-1, while the Vikings fell to 7-3. Minnesota's defense had entered the game allowing an NFL-best 14 points per game and a league-low 69.5 passer rating.

Beyond the scoreline, the matchup brought together two of the league's most outspoken Christian quarterbacks. Purdy is an outspoken Christian whose faith-shaped leadership was profiled on November 13, when he spoke of leaning on Romans 8:28 and mentoring under Pastor Joby Martin.

Cousins was profiled by Sports Spectrum on November 11 on returning to Minnesota knowing he is "sent by God" wherever he plays. On a night when the league crossed the border for its biggest weekly stage, the two men arrived in Mexico City carrying the same conviction: that football is a platform, not an identity.
