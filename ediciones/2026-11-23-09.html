Marvin Sapp's "Restoration" — his 17th album overall and his 9th live recording — was released November 20, 2026, via Roc Nation Distribution, the first full-length gospel album released by Roc Nation Distribution. It is his first full-length album since "Then and Now" (August 30, 2024).

Recorded live at The Chosen Vessel Cathedral in Fort Worth, Texas, where Sapp serves as senior pastor, the album has 10 tracks, including "You Are Welcome," "Best In Me," "The Blood," "At His Feet," "Never Let Me Go," "Victory Is in My Voice," "You're My Peace," the title track "Restoration," "Breathe Into Me" and "He's Not Finished."

After more than three decades recording for Sony labels, Sapp signed with Roc Nation Distribution — a partnership announced in July 2026 — in a deal that lets him retain ownership of his masters, making this the first album of his career he owns outright.

The album grew out of a bruising stretch Sapp says brought death threats and police protection at his church, following the viral "close the doors" backlash earlier in 2026. He supported the release with the Restoration Experience Tour, visiting 10 U.S. locations from November 10 to 19, from Jackson, Mississippi, to Cincinnati, Ohio.

A new concept video for "Never Let Me Go" premiered on YouTube on release day. After a year that tested him publicly, Sapp's answer is a live worship record — and, for the first time, one he owns.
