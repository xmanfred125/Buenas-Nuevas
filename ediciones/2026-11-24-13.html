"God With Us": the Faith Drama That Opened With $885,000 and Extended Its Christmas Run«God With Us»: el drama de fe que abrió con 885.000 dólares y extendió su cartelera navideña

Jimmy Jenkins's Nativity reimagined as a contemporary love story opened in 432 theaters on November 13, and strong audience response led distributor Blue Harbor Entertainment to extend its run through the Christmas season.La Natividad reimaginada como una historia de amor contemporánea, de Jimmy Jenkins, se estrenó el 13 de noviembre en 432 salas, y la respuesta del público llevó a la distribuidora Blue Harbor Entertainment a extender su cartelera durante toda la Navidad.

November 18, 202618 de noviembre de 2026

God With Us, a faith drama written and directed by Jimmy Jenkins, opened in theaters on November 13 in 432 theaters. Its opening weekend was reported at $885,000 according to the distributor's statement, while The Numbers records $860,488 as the actual box-office figure — a reminder that opening-weekend numbers for independent faith films often arrive in two versions, the distributor's and the tracker's.

Because of the audience response, distributor Blue Harbor Entertainment extended the theatrical run through the whole Christmas season. The strongest markets were Washington, D.C., Atlanta, Chicago, Baltimore and Philadelphia. In its second weekend, November 20, the film grossed $101,972 in 147 theaters, for a cumulative total of $1,104,728, according to The Numbers.

More than 4,000 people attended the premiere celebration at the First Baptist Church of Glenarden, in Upper Marlboro, Maryland, before the release — a sign of the church-based audience that has carried faith films for a generation.

The film reimagines the Nativity as a contemporary love story: childhood sweethearts Mary Anne Thomas and Joseph "Junie" Love, played by Ashley Olivia Fisher and Aspen Kennedy. The cast includes Lil Rel Howery, Rotimi, Clifton Powell, Roger Guenveur Smith and Gregory Alan Williams. Rotimi, also an executive producer, said the film holds a 98 percent score on Rotten Tomatoes.

God With Us arrives in a Christmas season crowded with streaming premieres and studio releases, and its extended run is a small victory for the kind of film that depends on word of mouth rather than marketing budgets. A Nativity told as a neighborhood love story, carried by churches and their congregations, will now play straight through Christmas.
