Twenty years later, the victory still belongs to God. Two-time Grammy Award-winning gospel artist Tye Tribbett released "V20: First Semester" on November 13, 2026 — a reimagining of his groundbreaking 2006 album "Victory" (Victory Live!), celebrating two decades since the record that helped redefine contemporary gospel's high-energy, choir-driven sound.

The project is the first installment of a two-part anniversary celebration; the second chapter is expected in 2027. It is released via Motown Gospel. The lead single, "I Want It All Back," featuring ABLAZE, the contemporary gospel youth movement, was released on October 9. The 16-track project revisits the original's songs with fresh arrangements and live instrumentation, including "Gimmie My Stuff Back (Reprise)," "Bless The Lord," "No Other Choice" featuring Thaddaeus Tribbett, and "Everything."

Tribbett said of the anniversary: "Twenty years later, the sound has grown, the testimony has deepened, and the victory still belongs to God! With V20 First Semester, we're celebrating everything Victory Live has meant to people while bringing that same faith and fire to a new generation."

The original "Victory" arrived in 2006 as a live album recorded with Tribbett's choir, Greater Anointing, and it became a landmark: gospel that was loud, joyful, sweaty and unapologetically Pentecostal, built for the sanctuary and the stage at once. Two decades on, the reimagining is not nostalgia. It is a test of whether the fire still burns — and, by Tribbett's account, the answer is that the sound has only grown.

El proyecto es la primera entrega de una celebración de aniversario en dos partes; la segunda se espera en 2027. Se publica a través de Motown Gospel. El sencillo principal, «I Want It All Back», con ABLAZE, el movimiento juvenil de gospel contemporáneo, se lanzó el 9 de octubre. El proyecto de 16 temas revisita las canciones del original con arreglos frescos e instrumentación en vivo, entre ellos «Gimmie My Stuff Back (Reprise)», «Bless The Lord», «No Other Choice» con Thaddaeus Tribbett y «Everything».

Tribbett dijo sobre el aniversario: «Veinte años después, el sonido ha crecido, el testimonio se ha profundizado ¡y la victoria sigue siendo de Dios! Con V20 First Semester celebramos todo lo que Victory Live ha significado para la gente, llevando esa misma fe y ese mismo fuego a una nueva generación».

El «Victory» original llegó en 2006 como un álbum en vivo grabado con el coro de Tribbett, Greater Anointing, y se convirtió en un hito: un gospel ruidoso, gozoso, sudoroso y sin complejos pentecostales, hecho a la vez para el santuario y para el escenario. Dos décadas después, la reinvención no es nostalgia. Es una prueba de si el fuego aún arde y, según Tribbett, la respuesta es que el sonido solo ha crecido.
