CeCe Winans Leads Gospel's Grammy Field With Three Nominations; Brandon Lake Earns TwoCeCe Winans lidera las nominaciones gospel del Grammy con tres; Brandon Lake suma dos

CeCe Winans leads the gospel and contemporary Christian field at the 69th Grammy Awards with three nominations, while Brandon Lake earns two — including a duet with country star Lainey Wilson.CeCe Winans lidera el campo gospel y cristiano contemporáneo de la 69.ª entrega de los Grammy con tres nominaciones, mientras Brandon Lake suma dos, incluido un dueto con la estrella country Lainey Wilson.

November 16, 202616 de noviembre de 2026

CeCe Winans leads the gospel and contemporary Christian field at the 69th Annual Grammy Awards with three nominations, announced Monday, November 16. Winans is nominated for "At The Cross" in Best Gospel Performance/Song, for The Hymns in Best Gospel Album, and for "Worthy Of It All (Worthy)" in Best Contemporary Christian Music Performance/Song. The ceremony is scheduled for February 7, 2027, in Los Angeles.

Brandon Lake follows with two nominations of his own: "The Jesus I Know Now," his duet with country star Lainey Wilson, in Best Contemporary Christian Music Performance/Song, and King of Hearts – In the Room! in Best Contemporary Christian Music Album. Across the five gospel and contemporary Christian categories, 26 entries earned nominations this year.

The field around them is deep. In gospel, Kirk Franklin and Karen Clark Sheard are nominated for "Thank You For Everything," alongside Cory Henry, Lisa Knowles-Smith & The Brown Singers, PJ Morton and Jonathan McReynolds. In contemporary Christian music, Lecrae is nominated for "Living Water," Elevation Worship and Chandler Moore for "God I'm Just Grateful," and Israel & New Breed for Coritos Vol. 2, with DOE, Jamie MacDonald and Tauren Wells rounding out the album category.

For Winans, the nominations add to one of the most decorated careers in gospel music: 18 Grammy wins and 35 nominations in total. But the wider story is the health of the field itself — worship, gospel and Christian hip-hop standing side by side on music's biggest stage, with songs about the cross and the hymns of the Church competing for the industry's highest honor.

Pero la historia más amplia es la salud del propio campo: adoración, gospel y hip-hop cristiano lado a lado en el escenario más grande de la música, con canciones sobre la cruz y los himnos de la Iglesia compitiendo por el máximo honor de la industria.
