Utah's Devon Dampier, a Heisman Contender, Wants 'to Be More Christlike'Devon Dampier, de Utah y candidato al Heisman, quiere 'ser más como Cristo'

Undefeated Utah's senior quarterback is a Heisman contender — but the grandson of a pastor says football is not his No. 1 priority.El mariscal de campo senior de Utah, invicto, es candidato al Heisman, pero el nieto de un pastor dice que el fútbol no es su prioridad número uno.

November 12, 202612 de noviembre de 2026

Devon Dampier did a little bit of everything in Utah's 28-20 win over in-state rival BYU last Saturday — and in doing so, he kept the Utes undefeated and firmly in the national title conversation. The senior quarterback threw for 177 yards and two touchdowns, rushed for 89 yards and two more scores, and even made an impressive 29-yard catch as No. 3 Utah defeated No. 9 BYU.

Utah is undefeated through nine games, ranked No. 2 nationally, and Dampier has emerged as one of the Big 12's top quarterbacks and a legitimate Heisman Trophy candidate. It is the kind of season that can define a career — and Dampier, who lists 'Child of God' in his Instagram bio, is determined that it will not define him.

Faith has been part of his life long before he arrived at Utah. His maternal grandfather is a pastor, and his parents, Curtis and Kesha, helped establish a foundation that continues to influence the way Dampier approaches life and football. 'I'm a Christian. I put all my faith into Christ and I think the biggest way that applies for me in football is just perspective, honestly,' he said. 'Football isn't my No. 1 priority. My relationship with God means everything to me.' What he wants, he has said, is simple: 'I want to be more Christlike.'

Utah travels to take on No. 23 Arizona this Saturday, and the stakes keep rising. But Dampier's measure of success was set long before the Heisman conversation began. If the Utes keep winning, the spotlight will only grow brighter — and the quarterback at the center of it intends to keep pointing it somewhere else.
