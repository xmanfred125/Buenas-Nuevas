In Liberia, 45,000 students have turned to Christ in a school evangelism movementEn Liberia, 45.000 estudiantes se han vuelto a Cristo en un movimiento de evangelización escolar

The ministry FORGE reports that some 45,000 young people have made decisions to follow Jesus over the past year, beginning with just 28 trained evangelists.El ministerio FORGE informa que unos 45.000 jóvenes han decidido seguir a Jesús durante el último año, empezando con apenas 28 evangelistas capacitados.

Abba Post · November 9, 2026Abba Post · 9 de noviembre de 2026

A remarkable student evangelism movement is unfolding in Liberia, where the Christian organization FORGE reports that approximately 45,000 young people have made decisions to follow Jesus over the past year.

The effort began with a group of just 28 trained evangelists who took the Gospel into schools and communities across the West African nation, combining evangelism with discipleship and follow-up so that new believers would be rooted in local churches rather than left on their own.

Ministry leaders say the movement shows how ordinary Christians can make an extraordinary difference when they commit themselves to sharing their faith. What started as a small team has multiplied as students themselves began telling classmates and friends about Christ, turning schools into fields of harvest.

The figure of 45,000 is reported by the ministry and has not been independently audited, but the scale of the response has drawn attention well beyond Liberia. CBN News featured the movement in a video report on Monday, highlighting the stories of young people whose lives have been changed.

Liberia, a country founded in the nineteenth century by freed American slaves and shaped deeply by Christian faith, has long been one of West Africa’s most churchgoing nations. For those praying for the next generation, the reports from its classrooms are a cause for thanksgiving — and for prayer that these young believers will keep growing in God’s Word and become witnesses in their own communities.
