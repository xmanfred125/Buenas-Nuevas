Perry's 'Gospel of Christmas' Brings a Gospel Dream Team to Netflix«The Gospel of Christmas», de Perry, lleva un equipo de ensueño del gospel a Netflix

Tyler Perry's "The Gospel of Christmas," starring Durrell "Tank" Babbs and Fantasia Barrino-Taylor with a gospel dream-team cast and music by Kirk Franklin, premiered in Atlanta and is now streaming on Netflix.«The Gospel of Christmas», de Tyler Perry, protagonizada por Durrell «Tank» Babbs y Fantasia Barrino-Taylor, con un reparto de ensueño del gospel y música de Kirk Franklin, se estrenó en Atlanta y ya está en Netflix.

November 11, 202611 de noviembre de 2026

Tyler Perry's "The Gospel of Christmas" arrived on Netflix on Wednesday, November 11, two days after its Atlanta premiere at the Tabernacle on Monday, November 9. Written, directed and produced by Perry, the film tells the story of Isaac — played by Durrell "Tank" Babbs — a former R&B singer turned pastor who tries to fulfil his late father's deathbed wish and lead his late grandfather's troubled church in a rundown New York City neighbourhood, after moving from Georgia with his wife Rebecca, played by Fantasia Barrino-Taylor, and their sons.

The film runs about one hour and 43 to 45 minutes and carries a TV-PG rating. But its most striking feature is the cast: a gospel dream team. CeCe Winans appears as Mrs. Johnson, Yolanda Adams as Gloria, Shirley Caesar as May, Fred Hammond as Abraham — Isaac's father — Ann Nesby as Sarah, Hezekiah Walker as Matthew, Smokie Norful as Peter and Jekalyn Carr as Lilly, alongside Le'Andria Johnson, Vanessa Bell Armstrong, Chanté Moore and members of The Clark Sisters.

The music, produced by Kirk Franklin, is woven through the story, including Winans singing "Goodness of God" and a rendition of Eartha Kitt's "Santa Baby." It is a Christmas film that leans into the sound of the Black church — not as background, but as the heartbeat of the narrative, carrying a story about grief, family, calling and a church in need of new life.

Perry, raised in the Baptist and African Methodist Episcopal traditions, told Religion News Service that he believes in angels and the devil, and that he is now writing a faith-centred television series. "The Gospel of Christmas" arrives as part of that same thread in his work: a story about a man caught between the stage he left behind and the pulpit he did not choose — and about a family learning, together, what it means to come home.

«The Gospel of Christmas», de Tyler Perry, llegó a Netflix el miércoles 11 de noviembre, dos días después de su estreno en Atlanta, en el Tabernacle, el lunes 9 de noviembre. La película dura alrededor de una hora y 43 a 45 minutos y tiene clasificación TV-PG. Pero su rasgo más llamativo es el reparto: un equipo de ensueño del gospel. CeCe Winans aparece como Mrs. Johnson, Yolanda Adams como Gloria, Shirley Caesar como May, Fred Hammond como Abraham —el padre de Isaac—, Ann Nesby como Sarah, Hezekiah Walker como Matthew, Smokie Norful como Peter y Jekalyn Carr como Lilly, junto a Le'Andria Johnson, Vanessa Bell Armstrong, Chanté Moore y miembros de The Clark Sisters.

La música, producida por Kirk Franklin, recorre toda la historia, e incluye a Winans cantando «Goodness of God» y una versión de «Santa Baby», de Eartha Kitt. Es una película navideña que se apoya en el sonido de la iglesia negra —no como fondo, sino como el latido de la narración—, para contar una historia de duelo, familia, llamado y una iglesia necesitada de nueva vida.

Perry, criado en las tradiciones bautista y metodista episcopal africana, dijo a Religion News Service que cree en los ángeles y en el diablo, y que ahora está escribiendo una serie de televisión centrada en la fe. «The Gospel of Christmas» llega como parte de ese mismo hilo en su obra: la historia de un hombre atrapado entre el escenario que dejó atrás y el púlpito que no eligió —y de una familia que aprende, junta, lo que significa volver a casa.
