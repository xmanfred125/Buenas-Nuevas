Forrest Frank's Record Tour Becomes ‘The Jesus Generation’ MovieLa gira récord de Forrest Frank se convierte en la película ‘The Jesus Generation’

Grammy-nominated artist Forrest Frank has released the first teaser for a documentary about his 2026 tour, which drew more than 519,000 people and became the biggest Christian music tour in history.El artista nominado al Grammy Forrest Frank lanzó el primer adelanto de un documental sobre su gira de 2026, que reunió a más de 519.000 personas y se convirtió en la gira de música cristiana más grande de la historia.

Abba Post · October 26, 2026Abba Post · 26 de octubre de 2026

The biggest Christian music tour in history is coming to movie theaters. Grammy-nominated artist Forrest Frank has released the first teaser for “The Jesus Generation,” a documentary about his record-setting 2026 tour, to be distributed by Lionsgate with Kingdom Story Company attached.

The 29-city Jesus Generation Tour ran from June 1 in Tulsa to a sold-out finale on August 1 at Globe Life Field in Arlington, Texas, before more than 35,000 people. In total it drew more than 519,000 people, averaging about 18,000 tickets a night and ranking No. 5 on Pollstar's LIVE75 chart.

Support acts included Tori Kelly, Cory Asbury and The Figs. The teaser mixes concert footage with prayer and testimony, with appearances from Chris Tomlin, Sadie Robertson Huff, Jelly Roll, Michael W. Smith, Rick Rubin and Philip Anthony Mitchell.

“I don't know a better name to call it than the Jesus generation,” Frank said of the tour and the film. The documentary is set for a theatrical release in 2027; no director or exact date has been announced yet, and fans are being directed to the artist's website for updates.

For Frank, the numbers tell only half the story. The tour's nights were built around worship, prayer and public testimony — the same ingredients the film now carries from the arena to the screen.
