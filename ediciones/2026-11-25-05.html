Prophet Uebert Angel Arrested Mid-Service in HarareArrestan al profeta Uebert Angel en pleno culto en Harare

Zimbabwean preacher Uebert Angel, 48, was handcuffed and taken from his Sunday service by armed police over accusations of indecent assault and sodomy, which have not been proven in court.El predicador zimbabuense Uebert Angel, de 48 años, fue esposado y sacado de su culto dominical por policías armados, por acusaciones de agresión indecente y sodomía que no han sido probadas en corte.

November 22, 202622 de noviembre de 2026

Uebert Angel — born Uebert Mudzanire, 48, founder of Spirit Embassy, also known as Good News Church, and a roving ambassador for Zimbabwe — was arrested during the Sunday service on November 22 at the Harare Hippodrome. About ten police officers carrying AK rifles entered the auditorium, handcuffed him, and took him to the Criminal Investigations Department division at police headquarters.

The Zimbabwe Republic Police confirmed the arrest over accusations of indecent assault and sodomy. Police have not said who the complainants are or when the alleged incidents are said to have taken place. The accusations have not been proven in court, and the presumption of innocence applies.

Spirit Embassy issued a statement on November 23 saying Angel is cooperating with the authorities and will answer through the courts. His lawyer, Lovemore Madhuku, said he could appear before a judge on Monday or Tuesday. It has not been confirmed whether that appearance took place.

The arrest comes two weeks after Angel publicly defended Vice President Constantino Chiwenga in a sermon, a detail that has fueled speculation in Zimbabwe about the timing — though no evidence has been presented linking the arrest to politics.

For Christians watching from afar, the case is a reminder of two truths that must be held together: leaders in the church are accountable before God and before the law, and every person accused of a crime deserves a fair hearing before being judged. The coming days in a Harare courtroom will say much about which of those truths Zimbabwe is prepared to honor.
