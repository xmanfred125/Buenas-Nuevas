'Drummer Boy' marches to a strong opening: Angel Studios' first live-action musical earns $3.5 million on Friday«Drummer Boy» marcha hacia un estreno fuerte: el primer musical de acción real de Angel Studios recauda 3,5 millones de dólares el viernes

Angel Studios has built its reputation on films that find their audience by word of mouth, and 'Drummer Boy' is shaping up as its latest test of that approach. The Revolutionary War musical hit theaters on Friday with a cast and crew led by Joel David Smallbone — of the Grammy-winning Christian pop group for KING + COUNTRY — who also co-directed. Early numbers suggest holiday-season audiences are turning out.

The film grossed an estimated $3.5 million on Friday, including previews, from 2,850 theaters. Projections point to a three-day opening weekend of $8 million to $11 million, which would make it one of the three biggest live-action debuts in Angel Studios' history. Audiences gave it an A CinemaScore, and on Rotten Tomatoes it holds a 93 percent audience score.

The story follows two brothers who end up on opposite sides of the American Revolutionary War. Smallbone stars as Jeremiah Clem, and Lucas Leach plays his brother Benjamin, who joins the Patriot forces as a military drummer. The film leans on the family's divided loyalties and the hope of reconciliation, and it folds in the Christmas carol that gives the film its title — the filmmakers pitched it as a Christmas musical that blends history, music and spectacle.

It is also a first for Angel Studios: the studio's first live-action theatrical musical, with a soundtrack that arrived in stores the same day as the film. The movie is rated PG-13 for war violence and some bloody images. Reviews so far are mixed but generally warm; Common Sense Media said the Christian themes are woven in organically without overshadowing an engaging story, though it called some of the history inaccurate.

For faith-based cinema, 'Drummer Boy' is another sign that the audience is there when the story is told with craft and conviction.
