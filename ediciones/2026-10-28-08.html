Indonesian Church Wins Building Permit After 12 Years Without a HomeUna iglesia indonesia obtiene permiso de construcción tras 12 años sin hogar

A congregation of about 30 people in Indonesia, forced to rotate services among homes for years, finally secured building permits after four years of applications.Una congregación de unas 30 personas en Indonesia, obligada durante años a rotar sus cultos entre hogares, finalmente obtuvo permisos de construcción tras cuatro años de solicitudes.

Abba Post · October 27, 2026Abba Post · 27 de octubre de 2026

After 12 years without a place of their own, a small Indonesian congregation finally has permission to build. The church of about 30 people, led by a pastor identified as “Andi,” secured building permits this month after four years of applications.

For years the congregation rotated its services among members' homes. Its story drew wider attention in 2022, when its lack of a building became public, but the permits still took years. Indonesian rules require support from at least 60 nearby residents, plus recommendations from the local interfaith forum and the religious affairs ministry.

International Christian Concern, which reported the breakthrough this week, has supported the congregation with Bibles, communion sets, a guitar and a keyboard — small tools for a church that has worshipped for years without a building to put them in.

The permit does not end the congregation's challenges, but it changes them. A church that met in living rooms can now plan walls, a roof and a door open to its neighbors.

For its members, the document is more than paperwork. It is the first official recognition, after more than a decade of waiting, that their church belongs where it is.
