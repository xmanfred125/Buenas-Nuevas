Kirk Cousins Is Back in Minnesota — and Still Following Jesus, Whatever It CostsKirk Cousins vuelve a Minnesota y sigue a Jesús, cueste lo que cueste

Traded back to Minnesota at the deadline, the four-time Pro Bowler returns to a 6-2 team — and to the question that has shaped his career since he was 17.Traspasado de vuelta a Minnesota en la fecha límite, el cuatro veces Pro Bowler regresa a un equipo de 6-2, y a la pregunta que ha marcado su carrera desde los 17 años.

November 11, 202611 de noviembre de 2026

One of the NFL's most outspoken Christians is returning to the team where he made his name — and where fans hope he can revive a struggling offense. Kirk Cousins was one of the biggest names traded at Tuesday's NFL deadline, going from the Las Vegas Raiders to a Minnesota Vikings team with one of the league's top defenses and an offense that has struggled to score in recent weeks.

Minnesota, at 6-2 and in first place in its division, is hoping to make a Super Bowl run. Cousins, a four-time Pro Bowl selection, played six seasons in Minnesota, the last of which was cut short by a torn Achilles tendon in 2023. This season marks his fifteenth in the NFL, his first with the Raiders, and in the season opener he reached 301 career touchdown passes.

Cousins has never hidden what anchors him. His Instagram profile reads simply: 'Believer. Husband. Father,' with Micah 6:8 underneath — 'And what does the LORD require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God.' In a video shared by Christhlete, he drew a line that has stayed with him since he was a teenager: 'There's a difference between being a fan of Jesus and being a follower of Jesus.'

He has said the question that shaped him came young: 'Are you prepared to follow Jesus even when it costs you something?' He decided at 16 or 17 that he would. On the Sports Spectrum podcast, he described his career as something he stewards rather than owns. 'Wherever you end up playing, you are sent by God, and He has a purpose for you being there,' he said. 'The first thing to do in being a steward is to surrender.' For Vikings fans, the hope is that the surrendered quarterback can still throw — and that a familiar face can carry a 6-2 team where it has not been in years.

'Dondequiera que termines jugando, Dios te ha enviado, y Él tiene un propósito para que estés allí', dijo. 'Lo primero que hay que hacer para ser un administrador es rendirse'. Para los aficionados de los Vikings, la esperanza es que el mariscal de campo rendido aún pueda lanzar, y que una cara conocida pueda llevar a un equipo de 6-2 a donde no ha estado en años.
