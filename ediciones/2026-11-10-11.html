Forrest Frank announces 'The Jesus Generation' album and answers his critics with faithForrest Frank anuncia el álbum «The Jesus Generation» y responde a sus críticos con fe

The Christian music star will release his new album on December 4, led by the single "Go! Go! Go!" with Lecrae and KB — and he is unapologetic about speaking his faith in public.La estrella de la música cristiana lanzará su nuevo álbum el 4 de diciembre, encabezado por el sencillo «Go! Go! Go!» con Lecrae y KB, y no se disculpa por hablar de su fe en público.

Forrest Frank has announced his new album, The Jesus Generation, releasing December 4 on River House Records. The project features collaborations with Lecrae, KB, Michael W. Smith and more, and its latest single, "Go! Go! Go!" — released Friday, November 6, with Lecrae and KB — is an upbeat, piano-driven rap anthem about trusting God and being ready to follow wherever He leads.

The album takes its name from Frank's May 2026 single "The Jesus Generation," which also gave its name to his summer 2026 tour — a 29-city stadium and arena run his team bills as the biggest Christian music tour in history. A film of the tour is headed to theaters in 2027 through Lionsgate and Kingdom Story Company. "Go! Go! Go!" follows the October 30 single "What a Life," and arrives alongside "JESUS IS ALIVE" with LOST & FOUND and "OKAY!" as part of a four-song preview of the album.

Frank, 30, has also been in the headlines for refusing to stay quiet about his faith. After speaking publicly about the assassination of conservative activist Charlie Kirk at Utah Valley University on September 10, he said tens of thousands of people unfollowed him on Instagram. "30,000 people unfollowed me because I posted about Charlie Kirk and I said Jesus Christ is Lord. Good. I don't want you to follow me," he said in a video, before debuting an emotional new song with the line "Jesus is coming back soon."

The singer also answered critics online after the midterm elections with a blunt message of his own: "Don't mess around with God." For Frank, the album's title is more than a brand — it is a statement about a generation he believes is choosing Jesus in public, whatever it costs.
