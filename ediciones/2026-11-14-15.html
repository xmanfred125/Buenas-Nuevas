'The Legacy Continues': A South African Gospel Voice Carries the Worship Forward«El legado continúa»: una voz del gospel sudafricano lleva la adoración adelante

Minister Lufuno Dagada has released 'The Legacy Continues,' a 13-track album drawn from his 'The Final Bow' live recording, celebrating worship, continuity and God's faithfulness.El ministro Lufuno Dagada lanzó «The Legacy Continues», un álbum de 13 canciones tomado de su grabación en vivo «The Final Bow», que celebra la adoración, la continuidad y la fidelidad de Dios.

November 13, 202613 de noviembre de 2026

South African gospel minister Lufuno Dagada has released his new album, The Legacy Continues, bringing the worship experience of his The Final Bow live recording to listeners everywhere. The 13-track project captures a special moment of praise and worship, allowing audiences to relive the atmosphere of the live recording.

The album's title carries its message: continuity, purpose and commitment to worship. It opens with “Asimuloro / No Eye Has Seen,” setting the tone for a collection that moves between energetic praise and reflective worship, and brings together different gospel voices, musical expressions and languages — a portrait of the diversity of South African Christian music.

Dagada's release lands in a season when gospel music from the continent is finding wider audiences, from Nigerian worship albums to South African live recordings that travel far beyond their home churches. The Legacy Continues is built for that moment: a live recording, not a studio construction, meant to be heard as it was sung.

For the congregations that filled The Final Bow, the album is a keepsake of a night they shared. For listeners meeting Dagada for the first time, it is an introduction to a worship tradition that pairs deep Scripture with full-voiced praise — and to a minister who sees his work as carrying forward a legacy rather than starting one.

The legacy, the album insists, does not belong to a single voice. It continues in every congregation that sings these songs after the recording ends — which is, after all, the point of a live worship album.

The Legacy Continues está construido para ese momento: una grabación en vivo, no una construcción de estudio, pensada para escucharse tal como se cantó.
