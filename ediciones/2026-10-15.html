The announcement came paired with a painful restructuring: roughly 20% of the overall staff and 40% of the editorial team were laid off, according to a MinistryWatch report. Among the departures is Andy Olsen, the award-winning news editorial director, and deputy editor Bonnie Kristian announced her resignation in the wake of the cuts.

Petrik becomes the sixth editor-in-chief in less than seven years — following Mark Galli, Daniel Harrell, Tim Dalrymple, Russell Moore and Marvin Olasky — a turnover that reflects years of financial strain and a shrinking readership. New president Nicole Martin now faces the task of steadying one of the most influential voices in the global evangelical movement.
