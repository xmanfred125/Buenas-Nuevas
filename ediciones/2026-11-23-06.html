Rev. Fred Nile, Australia's Longest-Serving MP and Christian Democratic Pioneer, Dies Aged 92El reverendo Fred Nile, el parlamentario con más años de servicio en Australia y pionero demócrata cristiano, muere a los 92 años

The Rev. the Hon. Frederick John Nile died peacefully on 21 November 2026, aged 92, his family confirmed in a statement on 22 November. He was the longest-serving parliamentarian in New South Wales history, sitting in the state's Legislative Council for more than 40 years, from his election in September 1981 until his retirement in 2023.

Nile was an ordained Congregational evangelical minister, ordained in 1964, and the founder, leader and national president of the Christian Democratic Party. He was a conservative Christian campaigner, vocal in parliament against abortion, same-sex marriage and gay adoption.

Born on 15 September 1934 in Kings Cross, Sydney, he volunteered for army service in 1954 before studying theology and becoming a minister. His decades in public life made him one of Australia's most recognisable Christian voices in politics.

In later years he supported legislation expunging historical homosexual convictions and worked on protecting First Nations sites — a reminder that his long record in the chamber was more varied than his reputation as a campaigner alone might suggest.

Australian Christians, in a statement on 22-23 November, described him as "a man of unwavering faith and a tireless servant of God and the people of New South Wales." For evangelical believers across Australia, his passing closes a chapter in which a pastor-preacher became the longest-serving legislator his state has ever known.
