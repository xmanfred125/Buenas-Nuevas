Fifteenth No. 1: Kirk Franklin Extends His Own Gospel Chart RecordDecimoquinto número 1: Kirk Franklin amplía su propio récord en las listas gospel

Kirk Franklin has done it again — and this time he has extended a record that was already his alone. His new album, "I Am What I Am," has reached No. 1 on Billboard's Top Gospel Albums chart, his 15th career No. 1 there, extending Billboard's record for the most No. 1 albums in the chart's history. The news was reported on November 11-12, 2026.

The album, released October 30, 2026 via Uptown Records, Fo Yo Soul and Tribl Records, is Franklin's 14th solo album and the first double album of his career: seven studio tracks — with GloRilla, Jelly Roll and Davido — plus seven songs recorded live in church. It also reached No. 1 on Apple Music's Christian Albums chart, No. 5 on iTunes Christian Albums and No. 8 on Luminate's Top Digital Album Sales.

The chart-topper follows a lead single that had already made history. "This Time," featuring GloRilla and released September 25, debuted at No. 1 on Billboard's Hot Gospel Songs chart on October 10 — Franklin's 10th No. 1 there, making him the first artist to reach double digits on that chart since 2005. The 20-time Grammy winner continues to set the pace for gospel music more than three decades into his career.

This No. 1 is a follow-up to the album's release two weeks earlier: the record itself was the news on October 30; now the chart position confirms its reception. A double album that pairs studio collaborations with the sound of a live church service has found its audience — and Franklin, once again, stands alone at the top of the chart he has defined.
