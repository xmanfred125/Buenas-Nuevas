A Nativity for the Inner City: 'God With Us' Opens for Three Days OnlyUna Natividad para el barrio: «God With Us» se estrena por solo tres días

Jimmy Jenkins' contemporary Nativity, "God With Us," opens today in select US cinemas for three days only, born from a detained teenager's question: "Can God forgive me?"La Natividad contemporánea de Jimmy Jenkins, «God With Us», se estrena hoy en cines seleccionados de Estados Unidos por solo tres días, nacida de la pregunta de un adolescente detenido: «¿Puede Dios perdonarme?».

November 13, 202613 de noviembre de 2026

A Nativity story set not in ancient Bethlehem but in a modern American inner city opens in select cinemas across the United States today, Friday, November 13, for a three-day run through Sunday, November 15. "God With Us," written and directed by Jimmy Jenkins, retells the birth of Christ through the lives of an African American community in the city — and it begins with a disclaimer: this is not an attempt to rewrite the Gospel, but a modern parable.

The film follows Mary Anne Thomas, played by Ashley Olivia Fisher, and Joseph "Junie" Love, played by Aspen Kennedy, as they navigate an unexpected pregnancy, doubt and danger in their neighborhood. The cast also includes Rotimi, Lil Rel Howery, Gregory Alan Williams, Roger Guenveur Smith, Clifton Powell and Ashley Rios. It is distributed by Blue Harbor Entertainment and Variance Films, with tickets available through godwithusmovie.com.

Jenkins is the son of Pastor John K. Jenkins Sr. of the First Baptist Church of Glenarden, and the film's origin lies in a moment from his own ministry. While preaching in a juvenile detention facility, a 16-year-old serving time for murder interrupted him: "God can't save us… we're murderers." Then came the question that stayed with him: "Can God forgive me?" That conversation became the seed of the film — a story about whether grace can reach the places society has written off.

The three-day release is a deliberate, event-style opening: Friday through Sunday only, in select cinemas. For Jenkins, the point is not novelty for its own sake but proximity — showing that the story of God coming to dwell among his people is not confined to a distant past or a faraway place, but speaks directly to the streets, the families and the young people of today.

Y luego llegó la pregunta que lo marcó: «¿Puede Dios perdonarme?». Esa conversación se convirtió en la semilla del filme: una historia sobre si la gracia puede alcanzar los lugares que la sociedad ha dado por perdidos.

El estreno de tres días es una apertura deliberada, pensada como evento: solo de viernes a domingo, en cines seleccionados. Para Jenkins, el punto no es la novedad por sí misma, sino la cercanía: mostrar que la historia de Dios viniendo a habitar entre su pueblo no pertenece a un pasado lejano ni a un lugar remoto, sino que habla directamente a las calles, las familias y los jóvenes de hoy.
