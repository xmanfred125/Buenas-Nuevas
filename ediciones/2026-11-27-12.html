1,143 Baptized in a Single Day in Peru as a Hundred Pastors Step Into the Waters Together1.143 bautizados en un solo día en Perú: un centenar de pastores descienden juntos a las aguas

November 26, 202626 de noviembre de 2026

Churches of the Movimiento Misionero Mundial in the Lima area gathered in Lurín, south of the Peruvian capital, where about 100 pastors baptized 1,030 people in a single day. With 113 more people from other congregations, the movement received 1,143 new members in all, according to the reports of the event. The baptisms took place during the week of November 23–26 and were reported on November 26.

Those baptized ranged from teenagers to elderly believers, some in wheelchairs. One moment stood out: Gabriel Espejo, 18, descended into the waters alongside his father and his grandfather, both pastors — three generations of one family entering the water together.

The Movimiento Misionero Mundial is a Pentecostal movement founded in Puerto Rico in 1963; in Peru it says it has some 1,800 churches across the country and its own television channel. The figures come from the movement's own reports, as summarized by the Christian news roundup that first carried the story; they have not been independently verified. Even so, by any count it is one of the largest single baptism days of the year in Latin America — and a reminder that, far from the headlines about decline, the church in Peru is still growing one baptism at a time.
