Chris Pratt's “Way of the Warrior Kid” Arrives for Thanksgiving — and He Talks Christian Music With His Son“Way of the Warrior Kid”, de Chris Pratt, llega para Acción de Gracias… y él habla de música cristiana con su hijo

Chris Pratt's new film, based on Jocko Willink's best-selling children's book, premieres today on Apple TV — and Pratt says he listens to Christian music with his son, from Mike Malagies to Lauren Daigle.La nueva película de Chris Pratt, basada en el libro infantil de Jocko Willink, se estrena hoy en Apple TV, y Pratt cuenta que escucha música cristiana con su hijo, desde Mike Malagies hasta Lauren Daigle.

November 25, 202625 de noviembre de 2026

“Way of the Warrior Kid,” starring Chris Pratt as Navy SEAL uncle Jake and Jude Hill — known for “Belfast” — as 11-year-old Marc, premieres November 25 on Apple TV. Directed by McG and written by Will Staples, the film is based on the first book, published in 2017, in retired Navy SEAL Jocko Willink's best-selling children's series. Willink himself appears in a cameo as Marc's martial-arts instructor, and Linda Cardellini plays Marc's mother, Sarah.

The story follows Marc, a bullied middle-schooler who spends the summer with his uncle, a Navy SEAL on medical leave after a hostage-rescue mission gone wrong. Through discipline, fitness and accountability, Marc gains confidence — and the film ends by arguing for kindness and empathy rather than a fight with the bully. The film runs 1 hour and 45 minutes and is rated PG-13.

Early reviews have been mixed. Critics have praised the warm relationship between Pratt and Hill and the film's message that confidence is earned through discipline and hard work, while others have found Pratt subdued and the story formulaic. The Cinema Group gave it 3.5 out of 5 stars; JoBlo and The Wrap were more negative, while Cinefied called it “a must-see.”

The faith angle came in an interview with CCM Magazine, published November 19, in which Pratt spoke about the film and about fatherhood. He said he listens to Christian music with his son, naming viral Christian hip-hop artist Mike Malagies, and adding: “I also love Zach Williams, for KING + COUNTRY. I know they have a new album out and a little Lauren Daigle thrown in there.” He also discussed fatherhood and positive role models with Jocko Willink.

For families gathering for Thanksgiving, the film offers a story about what a boy can become when an adult takes the time to train him — in body, in character and in kindness. And Pratt's own playlist at home suggests the lessons are not only on screen: the same music he listens to with his son is the sound of the faith he talks about in public.

Y la lista de reproducción de Pratt en casa sugiere que las lecciones no están solo en la pantalla: la misma música que escucha con su hijo es el sonido de la fe de la que habla en público.
