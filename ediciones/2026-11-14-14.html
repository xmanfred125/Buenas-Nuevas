'The Drought Is Over': LO Worship Sings Hope From 4,000 Women's Voices«La sequía terminó»: LO Worship canta esperanza con 4.000 voces de mujeres

Recorded live before more than 4,000 women at the sold-out LO Sister Conference, LO Worship's new album declares that God is at work even before His promises are seen.Grabado en vivo ante más de 4.000 mujeres en la conferencia LO Sister, con entradas agotadas, el nuevo álbum de LO Worship declara que Dios obra incluso antes de que se vean sus promesas.

November 6, 20266 de noviembre de 2026

LO Worship has released a new live album with a message for anyone in a dry season. The Drought Is Over (From LO Conference) came out Friday, November 6, through Integrity Music, recorded before more than 4,000 women at the sold-out LO Sister Conference earlier this year.

The nine-track project runs just over an hour and draws its theme from 1 Kings 18, the story of Elijah and the rain that ended a prolonged drought. The title track, featuring worship artist Anna Golden, declares that God is at work even before His promises are seen. The set also includes “The Perfect Love of Jesus” featuring Jenn Johnson, a new live version of the group's song “I Remind Myself,” and a message track, “A Thrill of Hope,” featuring Sadie Robertson Huff.

LO Worship is the worship collective that grew out of Live Original, the ministry led by Sadie Robertson Huff. The collective is made up of family members and lifelong friends from West Monroe, Louisiana, and the new album follows the group's 2025 debut full-length, Songs for the Anxious Heart, which dealt with anxiety, peace and identity in Christ.

The conference setting is part of the sound. The album captures the atmosphere of a room full of women worshipping together — not a studio-polished product but a live gathering, with all the warmth and imperfection that carries.

For listeners walking through their own drought — grief, waiting, unanswered prayer — the album's argument is simple and scriptural: the rain in Elijah's story came after the waiting, not before it. The drought is over, the song says, because God was already moving while the sky was still clear.

El repertorio también incluye «The Perfect Love of Jesus» con Jenn Johnson, una nueva versión en vivo de la canción del grupo «I Remind Myself» y un mensaje, «A Thrill of Hope», con Sadie Robertson Huff.

El colectivo está formado por familiares y amigas de toda la vida de West Monroe, Luisiana, y el nuevo álbum sigue al debut del grupo en 2025, Songs for the Anxious Heart, que trató sobre la ansiedad, la paz y la identidad en Cristo.

El ambiente de la conferencia forma parte del sonido. El álbum captura la atmósfera de una sala llena de mujeres adorando juntas: no un producto pulido de estudio, sino una reunión en vivo, con toda la calidez y la imperfección que eso conlleva.

Para quienes atraviesan su propia sequía —duelo, espera, oración sin respuesta—, el argumento del álbum es sencillo y bíblico: la lluvia en la historia de Elías llegó después de la espera, no antes. La sequía terminó, dice la canción, porque Dios ya se estaba moviendo mientras el cielo aún estaba despejado.
