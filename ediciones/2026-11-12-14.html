A New Orleans Christmas: Lauren Daigle's new label, new soundUna Navidad de Nueva Orleans: Lauren Daigle estrena disquera y sonido

Two-time Grammy winner Lauren Daigle has a new label, a new sound for Christmas, and a homecoming to celebrate. The Louisiana singer has signed with Capitol, Interscope and Capitol Christian Music Group, and her debut for the new home is two Christmas songs recorded with the Preservation Hall Jazz Band of New Orleans: “Sleigh Ride” and “Go Tell It On The Mountain.” The songs arrived on digital platforms on October 30, with a seven-inch vinyl edition coming November 27.

For Daigle, who has passed a billion streams worldwide and whose song “You Say” is certified six-times platinum, the recording was a return to her roots. She called it a “full circle” moment: a girl from Louisiana, singing the songs of a New Orleans Christmas with the band that has carried the city's jazz tradition for generations. Her first Christmas album, Behold, came out in 2016; a decade later, she is singing the season again, with the sound of home behind her.

The celebration continues on stage. The tenth annual Behold Christmas Tour opens December 2 and 3 at the Ryman Auditorium in Nashville, then comes to New Orleans' Saenger Theatre on December 5 and 6 with the Preservation Hall Jazz Band, and closes December 18 in Orlando. For fans who have followed Daigle from worship stages to pop radio and back, the tour is a reminder of where she started — and of the sound she never left behind.

Christmas, in Daigle's hands, is not a genre. It is a homecoming: brass and piano, a familiar hymn, and a voice that sounds like it is singing to the room it grew up in.
