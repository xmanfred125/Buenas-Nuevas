Sent by God: Kirk Cousins returns to the Vikings for a playoff pushEnviado por Dios: Kirk Cousins regresa a los Vikings para la lucha por los playoffs

Traded back to Minnesota at the deadline, Kirk Cousins returns to the team he led for six seasons — and to the stadium where his Achilles tore — saying wherever he plays, he was sent by God.Traspasado de vuelta a Minnesota en el cierre del plazo, Kirk Cousins regresa al equipo que lideró durante seis temporadas, y al estadio donde se rompió el Aquiles, diciendo que dondequiera que juegue, fue enviado por Dios.

Abba Post · November 10, 2026Abba Post · 10 de noviembre de 2026

Kirk Cousins is going home. On Tuesday, November 10, hours before the NFL's 4 p.m. trade deadline, the Las Vegas Raiders traded the 38-year-old quarterback back to the Minnesota Vikings — the team he led for six seasons — for a third-round pick in the 2027 draft.

Cousins spent 2018 to 2023 in Minnesota, starting 88 games, going 50-37-1, throwing 171 touchdowns against 55 interceptions and earning three Pro Bowl selections with the Vikings, four in total. He returns to a team that is 6-2 and first in its division, but reeling: on Monday night, the Vikings lost 23-6 to Buffalo without scoring a touchdown. He is expected to make his debut this Sunday, November 15, at 1 p.m. Eastern, at Green Bay — in the very stadium where he tore his Achilles tendon in October 2023.

For Cousins, one of the most openly Christian players in the NFL, the move is not a transaction. It is a calling. On the Sports Spectrum podcast in June 2026 he said: “Wherever you end up playing, you are sent by God, and He has a purpose for you being there.” His Instagram profile reads simply “Believer. Husband. Father,” with a reference to Micah 6:8, and he listens to worship music before games. “It's His career that I'm stewarding… first is surrender,” he said.

The business of the deal is straightforward. Cousins signed a one-year, 11.3-million-dollar contract with the Raiders in April 2026; the Raiders paid only about 500,000 to 650,000 dollars of his 1.3-million-dollar base salary for this season, and Minnesota assumes the rest, plus a 10-million-dollar bonus due in March 2027. With Las Vegas he threw 20 touchdowns and eight interceptions in nine games. His departure opens the way for rookie Fernando Mendoza, the number one pick of the 2026 draft and the Heisman Trophy winner.

The Vikings are betting that a familiar face, in a familiar place, can steady a season that still has everything to play for. Cousins, for his part, has already said where he believes he was sent — and why.
