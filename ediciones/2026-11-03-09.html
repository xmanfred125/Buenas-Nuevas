Netflix will release The Gospel of Christmas, Tyler Perry's faith-driven holiday drama, on Wednesday, November 11, in a release that gathers one of the deepest benches of gospel talent any streaming film has assembled this decade.

Perry wrote and directed the film, which follows Isaac, a struggling pastor who inherits his family's church in a troubled New York City neighborhood. Fantasia Barrino Taylor plays Rebecca, and R&B singer Durrell 'Tank' Babbs plays Isaac.

The cast reads like a praise and worship who's who: CeCe Winans, Yolanda Adams, Fred Hammond, Shirley Caesar, Jekalyn Carr, Smokie Norful, Hezekiah Walker, Le'Andria Johnson, Vanessa Bell Armstrong, Ann Nesby and Chanté Moore, with Ann Nesby portraying Sarah. The story centers on faith, family, perseverance and the power of belief as Isaac works to rebuild a failing church.

Kirk Franklin oversees the music and produces the soundtrack, blending traditional gospel, rap and holiday songs to give the film both a sermon-and-choir resonance and a modern pulse.

The film is the second title under the multiyear, first-look deal Perry signed with producer DeVon Franklin and Netflix in 2024 to make faith-based films, following last year's Ruth and Boaz — a sign that explicitly Christian storytelling has found a steady home on the world's largest streaming platform.
