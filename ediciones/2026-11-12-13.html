Three kings, one star: The Chosen's Christmas special adds Taye DiggsTres reyes y una estrella: el especial navideño de The Chosen suma a Taye Diggs

Taye Diggs will play Balthazar in The Chosen's third Christmas special, premiering on FOX on December 14 — as the series' sixth season arrives on Prime Video this Sunday.Taye Diggs interpretará a Baltasar en el tercer especial navideño de The Chosen, que se estrena en FOX el 14 de diciembre, mientras la sexta temporada de la serie llega a Prime Video este domingo.

FOX has announced the third Christmas special from The Chosen — and this one brings a new face to the manger. Actor Taye Diggs will play Balthazar in The Chosen: The Three Kings, alongside Piter Marek as Melchior and Rahul Rai as Gaspar. The special will premiere on FOX on December 14 at 8 p.m. Eastern, arriving on Hulu the following day.

The special follows the three wise men on their journey to Bethlehem after a sign in the heavens, and it is executive produced by Dallas Jenkins, the creator of the series. The casting news, announced November 11, is a new development for a franchise that has spent the week in the headlines: the sixth season of The Chosen premieres on Prime Video on Sunday, November 15, with its first three episodes, then weekly, covering the final 24 hours of Jesus' life, with the finale set to open in cinemas as a film on March 12, 2027.

The actors at the center of that season have been talking this week about what it cost to film it. Jonathan Roumie, who plays Jesus, Luke Dimyan, who plays Judas, and Andrew James Allen, who plays Pilate, all gave interviews published on November 11 about a season that asks its cast — and its audience — to sit with the hardest hours of the story.

For a series that began as a crowdfunded streaming experiment, the Christmas special is a measure of how far it has travelled: from a phone screen to a broadcast network's December schedule, telling the oldest journey in the Christmas story to a new audience, one king at a time.
