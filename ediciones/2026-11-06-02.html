'Drummer Boy' opens in theaters today: a Revolutionary War musical about two brothers and forgiveness'Drummer Boy' llega hoy a los cines: un musical de la Guerra de Independencia sobre dos hermanos y el perdón

Angel Studios releases 'Drummer Boy' in theaters nationwide today, November 6, 2026, in roughly 2,850 theaters. The historical musical drama is set during the American Revolutionary War and follows two brothers, Jeremiah Clem (Joel David Smallbone) and Benjamin Clem (Lucas Leach), who end up fighting on opposite sides of the conflict, alongside an enslaved woman, Emmaline (India Brown), who also searches for freedom.

The film is co-directed by brothers Ben Smallbone and Joel Smallbone of for KING + COUNTRY, with a screenplay by Richard L. Ramsey and music by the duo. The cast also includes Ioan Gruffudd, Melora Hardin, Charles Esten and Iain Glen. It is rated PG-13 for war violence and some bloody images, and runs 2 hours and 7 minutes. Angel calls it its first live-action theatrical musical; the brothers describe the project as eight years in the making.

At the heart of the film is forgiveness. Ben Smallbone told The Christian Post that a tearful coffee-shop reunion and mutual apology with his brother Joel inspired the story. The original spark was the Christmas song 'Little Drummer Boy,' which the brothers reimagined as a story of family, war and reconciliation.

The film follows the Smallbones' 2024 hit 'Unsung Hero,' which earned 21.1 million dollars at the box office on a 6 million dollar budget. A review published November 5 by Movieguide praised 'Drummer Boy' for its strong Christian worldview — characters pray, attend church and discuss their faith — and for its high production value.
