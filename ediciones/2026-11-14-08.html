310 Students, 24 Countries, Zero Tuition: A Free Bible College Goes Global310 estudiantes, 24 países, cero matrícula: una escuela bíblica gratuita se vuelve global

November 11, 202611 de noviembre de 2026

On September 18, 2026, Revelation Bible College & Seminary opened its doors online. Fifty-seven days later, 310 students from 24 countries had enrolled to study the Word of God — completely free.

The college began in Lahore, Pakistan, in 2018, training pastors and church workers in person. Pakistan remains at the heart of the school, with 143 students. But the online opening turned a local ministry into a global classroom: 150 students now come from 14 African countries, including 36 from Nigeria, 30 from Kenya, 27 from Zambia and 19 from Malawi, and 152 from six countries across Asia and the Pacific.

The model is deliberately simple. There is no tuition, no admissions barrier beyond a willingness to study, and the teaching is aimed at equipping ordinary believers — pastors, church workers and laypeople — to handle Scripture and lead others. The college's founder and president, the Rev. Dr. Naeem Nasir, describes the mission as taking the training that once required a plane ticket and putting it on a phone screen.

The speed of the growth raises its own questions — how to mentor 310 students across 24 time zones, and how to keep a free program sustainable — but the demand itself is the story. In countries where a seminary education is often out of reach, hundreds of students signed up in weeks.

For the churches those students come from, the arithmetic is hopeful: every graduate is a potential teacher, and every teacher multiplies. A college that began in one city in Pakistan now has a classroom that stretches from Lahore to Lusaka — and it charges nothing to enter.
