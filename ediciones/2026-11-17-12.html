YouVersion CEO Warns AI Chatbots Are Misquoting the BibleEl director de YouVersion advierte que los chatbots de IA citan mal la Biblia

Bobby Gruenewald told Christianity Today that ChatGPT, Claude and Google Gemini misquote Scripture — and revealed YouVersion shelved its own Bible chatbot years ago over accuracy and safety fears.Bobby Gruenewald dijo a Christianity Today que ChatGPT, Claude y Google Gemini citan mal las Escrituras, y reveló que YouVersion archivó su propio chatbot bíblico hace años por temor a errores y riesgos.

November 16, 202616 de noviembre de 2026

The biggest problem facing the world's most-used Bible app is not distribution but accuracy — and it comes from somewhere else. Bobby Gruenewald, founder and CEO of YouVersion, told Christianity Today on November 16 that ChatGPT, Claude and Google Gemini are misquoting the Bible, and that the errors are now the platform's main concern.

The Bible App has passed one billion downloads, and Gruenewald was in New York presenting a Times Square advertising campaign for Global Bible Month, a November initiative inviting people to spend more time in Scripture. Yet his warning was about what happens when people ask AI, rather than the Bible itself, for answers.

YouVersion learned the lesson early. Four or five years ago it built a prototype Bible chatbot — and decided not to launch it. The team could not guarantee accuracy, and it worried about people in crisis, including those with suicidal thoughts, treating a chatbot as an oracle with spiritual authority.

Now YouVersion is building a benchmark to measure how accurately the major models quote the Bible, and is in conversation with the labs behind them — OpenAI, Anthropic and Google — to correct the problem. The platform has cited the Bible accurately for more than 18 years, and Gruenewald wants the new tools held to the same standard.

The interview also offered a striking measure of the moment: half of the Christian questions reaching ChatGPT are about Bible interpretation. Millions of people are now asking machines to explain Scripture — which makes getting the words right not a technical detail, but a matter of spiritual care.
