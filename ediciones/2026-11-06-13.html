Seven-time Grammy winner TobyMac has released 'The Hard Times,' his first new single since his chart-topping 2025 album 'Heaven On My Mind.' The song was released October 30, 2026, through Capitol CMG.

The single was written by TobyMac (Kevin McKeehan), Dave Lubben and Micah Kuiper, and produced by Kuiper and McKeehan. Its message is hope and perseverance: life's hardest seasons can become opportunities for spiritual growth and renewed faith, acknowledging the weight of difficult experiences while pointing to the growth that can emerge through them.

'Heaven On My Mind' debuted at No. 1 on Billboard's Top Christian Albums chart and generated more than 5.1 million global streams in its opening week; an expanded 'Guest List Edition' came out on June 26, 2026.

TobyMac is touring this fall with Third Day and Chris Tomlin, and he launches the Hits Deep 2027 tour on January 28, 2027, joined by Jeremy Camp and Seph Schlueter.
