Christian singer Forrest Frank is warning progressives not to mess around with God, after viral posts celebrated the Democratic sweep in the 2026 midterm elections by promising to invent new genders. In posts shared this week, Frank answered the mockery directly — and made clear he knows the stand could cost him followers.

Frank, one of the most-streamed Christian artists of his generation, has built his audience on songs of joy, gratitude and simple faith. His response this week was in that same spirit: not a political argument, but a warning rooted in reverence. God is not a punchline, he said in effect, and those who treat Him as one should be careful.

The exchange captures a moment many Christian artists now face: a culture that rewards silence about faith and punishes those who speak plainly. Frank chose to speak plainly anyway, and his fans answered with the kind of loyalty that no algorithm can manufacture.

Whether the posts cost him streams or gain him new listeners, the message stands on its own. 'Don't mess around with God' is not a slogan; it is the oldest wisdom in the Bible, from the Proverbs to the prophets: the fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and those who forget it do so at their own risk.
