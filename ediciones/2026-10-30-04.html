Forrest Frank Announces 'The Jesus Generation' Album for December and a 2027 FilmForrest Frank anuncia el álbum 'The Jesus Generation' para diciembre y una película para 2027

Nashville, Tennessee — Dove Award-winning Christian artist Forrest Frank is expanding his 'Jesus Generation' movement with a new album and a forthcoming theatrical film, following a sold-out summer tour that drew tens of thousands of fans to arenas and stadiums across the United States.

Frank announced that his latest album, 'The Jesus Generation,' will arrive December 4 through River House Records and feature collaborations with Lecrae, KB, Michael W. Smith, JVKE, ZVC and Elevation Rhythm. Ahead of the release, the 31-year-old turned to his nearly 7 million Instagram followers to help choose the project's next single, sharing previews of nine songs and inviting fans to vote on which track should debut on Friday, October 30.

The album takes its name from Frank's May 2026 single 'The Jesus Generation,' a song reflecting his belief that a spiritual awakening is taking place across the country, particularly among young people. A news release said the record 'continues Forrest's social commentary on the country's current spiritual revival.'

The announcement builds on a historic summer. Frank's sold-out, 29-city Jesus Generation Tour, which ran from June 1 at the BOK Center in Tulsa, Oklahoma, to a sold-out finale before more than 35,000 people at Globe Life Field in Arlington, Texas, welcomed more than 519,000 fans in two months. The run debuted at No. 5 on Pollstar's LIVE75 chart and No. 12 on its Global Concert Pulse chart, and promoters have described it as the biggest tour in Christian music history.

Frank also revealed on October 23 that the tour will reach the big screen in 'The Jesus Generation Movie,' a theatrical release from Lionsgate and Kingdom Story Company scheduled for 2027. A teaser trailer mixes concert footage with behind-the-scenes moments and personal testimony, and features appearances by figures including Rick Rubin, Philip Anthony Mitchell, Sadie Robertson Huff, Chris Tomlin, Michael W. Smith and Jelly Roll. Pastor JP Pokluda of Harris Creek Baptist Church in Waco, Texas, served as the tour's evangelist, delivering a Gospel message at each stop.
