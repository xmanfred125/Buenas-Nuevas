One of the world's oldest Pentecostal denominations gathered in St. Louis this week for its 118th Annual Holy Convocation. The Church of God in Christ (COGIC) met from November 3 to 10, 2026, at the America's Center Convention Complex, under the theme 'Answering The Call', drawn from Acts 16:9, and led by Presiding Bishop J. Drew Sheard.

Sunday, November 8, was the official day of the convocation, featuring Bishop Sheard's official message. Gospel artist Kierra Sheard-Kelly ministered that day as well, in a programme that also included a Women's Day on November 6, a Consecration Service and World Youth Night on November 7, along with worship services, music, auxiliary meetings and official business sessions.

Delegates came from all 50 U.S. states and from around the globe — a measure of a denomination that now serves more than 12,000 churches in 106 countries. For many of those present, the convocation is both a spiritual homecoming and the place where the business of a global church gets done.

The gathering also matters to the city that hosts it. The convocation is projected to generate 250 million dollars in regional economic impact over three years, a reminder that a week of worship, music and fellowship can also be a significant blessing to the community that welcomes it.

Drew Sheard.

El domingo 8 de noviembre fue el día oficial de la convocación, con el mensaje oficial del obispo Sheard. La artista de gospel Kierra Sheard-Kelly también ministró ese día, en un programa que incluyó además un Día de la Mujer el 6 de noviembre, un Servicio de Consagración y una Noche Mundial de la Juventud el 7 de noviembre, junto con cultos, música, reuniones auxiliares y sesiones oficiales de trabajo.

Los delegados llegaron de los 50 estados de EE. UU. y de todo el mundo, una muestra de una denominación que hoy sirve a más de 12.000 iglesias en 106 países.
