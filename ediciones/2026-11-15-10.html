'God With Us' Brings the Nativity to a Modern Neighborhood — for Three Days Only'God With Us' lleva la Natividad a un barrio moderno, solo por tres días

Jimmy Jenkins's modern Nativity, set in a Maryland neighborhood, plays in select theaters for one weekend only — and is betting on church groups to fill the seats.La Natividad moderna de Jimmy Jenkins, ambientada en un barrio de Maryland, se proyecta en cines selectos por un solo fin de semana, apostando a que los grupos de iglesia llenen las salas.

November 13, 202613 de noviembre de 2026

A modern Nativity has come to the big screen — and only for three days. 'God With Us,' a new film from writer-director Jimmy Jenkins, opened Friday in select theaters for a limited run through Sunday, November 13 to 15, with no announced second weekend or streaming date.

The film moves the story of Mary and Joseph to a Black neighborhood in Maryland and sets the Nativity in the present day. It runs 105 minutes and is not rated. Blue Harbor Entertainment is the distributor, with Variance Films handling theatrical booking. The film is Jenkins's second feature after 'Sinners Wanted,' and it premiered on October 2 at First Baptist Church of Glenarden, where Jenkins's father, John K. Jenkins Sr., serves as senior pastor.

The three-day model is a deliberate choice. Rather than a wide release, the film's own group-sales page is selling opening-weekend tickets to churches and organizations, betting that congregations will fill seats together. It is a different path from the one Angel Studios is running this fall: 'Drummer Boy' opened in 2,850 theaters and grossed $9.4 million in its first weekend, and 'Hershey' is forecast at $4 million to $6 million for its Thanksgiving frame.

For audiences who want to see it, the window is short — and that is the point. 'God With Us' asks its viewers to meet the Christmas story not in a distant stable, but on a street they might recognize, in a neighborhood like their own. Whether the experiment works will be measured not in screen counts, but in sold-out rooms and church groups that decide the story is worth gathering around.

El modelo de tres días es una decisión deliberada. En lugar de un estreno amplio, la propia página de ventas grupales de la película vende entradas para el fin de semana de estreno a iglesias y organizaciones, apostando a que las congregaciones llenarán las salas juntas. Es un camino distinto al que recorre Angel Studios este otoño: 'Drummer Boy' se estrenó en 2.850 cines y recaudó 9,4 millones de dólares en su primer fin de semana, y se prevé que 'Hershey' recaude entre 4 y 6 millones en el fin de semana de Acción de Gracias.

Para quienes quieran verla, la ventana es corta, y ese es el punto. 'God With Us' pide a sus espectadores encontrarse con la historia de Navidad no en un establo lejano, sino en una calle que podrían reconocer, en un barrio como el suyo. Si el experimento funciona no se medirá en número de pantallas, sino en salas llenas y grupos de iglesia que decidan que la historia vale la pena reunirse a su alrededor.
